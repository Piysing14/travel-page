My Travel Memories

Mountain Adventures

Visiting Mussoorie in March 2023 was an unforgettable experience. The trek to George Everest and the charming streets of Landour were highlights. The breathtaking views and fun-filled moments made this trip one to remember.

📍 Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

Beachside Sunset

My visit to Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, was a rejuvenating experience. The trip was filled with beachside sunsets and reconnecting with friends. It was a perfect blend of relaxation and joy.

📍 Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

City of Lakes

Nainital’s serene lake, surrounded by green hills, provided an escape from city life. Boating on the misty lake and exploring the vibrant Mall Road were unforgettable moments.

📍 Nainital, Uttarakhand

Rafting at Rishikesh

Rafting on the Ganges at Rishikesh was an exhilarating adventure. The mix of thrilling rapids and peaceful ghats, combined with the Ganga Aarti, created a unique spiritual experience.

📍 Rishikesh, Uttarakhand

Highest Shiv Temple

The trek to Tungnath Mahadev, the highest Shiva temple, was a divine experience. Surrounded by nature, the serene atmosphere and spiritual energy were both healing and uplifting.

📍 Chopta, Uttarakhand

2025 @ Nainital

visited Nainital with friends, and the trip evoked old memories (IYKYK). We rang in the New Year on Mall Road, hoping that starting the year in the mountains would bring peace and strength, much like the mountains.
